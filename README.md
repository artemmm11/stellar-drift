# Stellar Drift

Free flight through a procedural star system, right in the browser.

**Open it:** https://artemmm11.github.io/stellar-drift/

The Elsvet system has a ringed gas giant (Morven) whose icy rings you can fly through, Perigee Station, the Weaver Nebula, Comet Iva with separate ion and dust tails, and Acheron, a black hole rendered by tracing light rays through curved spacetime.

Everything is generated by code when the page loads. No textures or models are downloaded. The whole scene is one `index.html` file (Three.js, WebGL 2), and it works on phones.

## The black hole

Acheron is ray-traced per pixel in a fragment shader:

- Photon paths follow the Schwarzschild geodesic equation, integrated with velocity Verlet and finer steps near the photon sphere, so the shadow, the thin photon ring and the lensed image of the far side of the disk come out of the physics rather than being drawn in.
- The accretion disk starts at the innermost stable circular orbit (3 Schwarzschild radii) and uses the thin-disk temperature profile T ∝ r^-3/4 (1 − √(r_in/r))^1/4.
- Each point of the disk is shifted by g = √(1 − 3M/r) / (1 − Ω L_z), which combines Doppler shift, time dilation and gravitational redshift. Brightness scales as g⁴ and color follows the shifted blackbody temperature. The side moving toward you is brighter and bluer, and the side moving away is dimmer and redder.

Because all of this is computed for the actual camera position, the hole looks right from any angle: a thin line with a lensed halo edge-on, a lopsided ring at moderate angles, and a symmetric ring face-on. The HUD shows your distance in Schwarzschild radii, how much slower clocks run there, and your angle above or below the disk.

## Controls

| Desktop | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | fly |
| Q / E | down / up |
| Shift | boost |
| Mouse | look (click the scene first) |
| 1–6 | viewpoints |
| T | auto tour |
| M | sound |
| H | telemetry |
| Esc | menu |

On a phone, use the left pad to fly and drag anywhere to look. The ▲ ▼ buttons move you up and down, BOOST speeds you up, and the 1–6 buttons fly to viewpoints. Tour and sound buttons are in the top-right corner.

If the frame rate stays low, the page lowers its rendering resolution automatically. You can also pick a detail level in the menu.
